# Changelog

## 2026-08-28 - Screenshots
- README gallery: Stormwind Harbor boat pin, Deadmines dungeon tooltip,
  Molten Core raid tooltip, Emeriss world boss pin.

## 2026-08-28 - Dungeons, raids and world bosses (v1.0)
- Three new pin categories with map filter and slash support.
- 32 dungeon entrances: every open 5-man plus the custom dungeons
  (Crescent Grove, Gilneas City, Hateforge Quarry, Karazhan Crypt,
  Stormwind Vault, Black Morass, Dragonmaw Retreat, Frostmane Hollow,
  Windhorn Caverns, Stormwrought Ruins). Entrances anchored to meeting
  stone spawns from the server database; level ranges taken from the
  in-game group finder's own data. Clicking a dungeon pin opens the
  group finder (LFT).
- 3 open raids (Molten Core, Onyxia's Lair, Lower Karazhan Halls) with
  attunement notes. Raids on the roadmap but not yet open are excluded
  until they unlock.
- 9 world bosses at their database spawn points: Azuregos, Lord Kazzak,
  the four Emerald dragons, Nerubian Overseer, Dark Reaver of Karazhan,
  Concavius.
- Full audit pass: Steelwing pinned at Stormwind Harbor (his one-way
  flight to Balor's SI:7 Outpost), Shimmerstar Lake flight point added,
  the Icepoint crossing corrected to a canoe ride, Rut'theran pier
  moved onto the boat's actual stop.

## 2026-08-28 - Verification updates
- Every boat and zeppelin route verified against the client's own
  transport path data; dock pins moved onto the actual dock stops
  (Stormwind Harbor southernmost pier, Windrunner Pier, Sparkwater,
  Kargath, Shadowprey, Moonhoof).
- Thunder Bluff zeppelin tower found and pinned; the new Orgrimmar
  tower moved to its real spot west of the city.
- Flight path unlock conditions added from the official OctoWoW
  transport page (Dun Agrath, Mosh'ogg Refuge, Caelan's Rest,
  Ironforge Airfield).
- Two Moonwhisper Coast flight masters added.
- Removed pins that turned out to be scenery, not services: three
  ambient horses (one player-tested), the Everlook display ram, and
  the flying gryphon/wyvern rentals that patch 1.18.0 removed from
  the game.

## 2026-08-27 - First release
- 162 pins across six categories: boats, zeppelins, the Deeprun Tram,
  99 flight masters, rentable mounts, portals and teleporters.
- World map pins with destination, crossing time, frequency and price
  tooltips; minimap pins; Travel button with category filters; custom
  pins via /otr add.
